Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy covers the applications YouTube-Uploader, Gaming-Uploader and CTI-Uploader (the "Apps"), personal tools operated by Haseeb Akbar.

Who uses the Apps

The Apps are used only by the owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels. They are not offered to other users.

Google user data accessed

The Apps request the YouTube Data API scopes needed to upload videos and set video metadata and thumbnails on the signed-in account's channel. They may read basic channel information to confirm the correct channel.

How data is used

Data is used only to upload the owner's videos and manage their metadata. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens are stored privately in the owner's own environment and are never published. No other personal data is collected or stored.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access makes stored tokens unusable; the owner deletes them on request.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The Apps' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The Apps use YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
